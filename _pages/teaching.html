Teaching philosophy

I emphasize in class discussion, debate and critical thinking. I focus on strengthening mathematical fundamentals and de-mystifying the language of economics for accessibility to students. I take a collaborative approach to AI use, where I support students in learning practical use cases for AI tools when supplemental support could be needed while emphasizing the core value add of critical thinking stemming from reading and handwriting notes. Check out my sample syllabus with principles of community and AI policy.
